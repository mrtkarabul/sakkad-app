Sakkad is an RSVP (Rapid Serial Visual Presentation) reader that speeds up your reading by showing words one at a time, fixed at the exact point where your eye focuses. Because your eye never jumps along the line, your attention holds — and your reading speed multiplies.

It takes its name from the scientific term for the jumping motion your eye makes while reading (the saccade) — the very thing this app eliminates.

Sakkad works fully offline: no accounts, no ads, no tracking. Your texts live on your device alone.
